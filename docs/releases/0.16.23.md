# Signal Outpost 0.16.23

## Scan analog and digital memories together

- Scan selected analog and digital conventional memories in one sequence with a single RTL-SDR.
- Switch between the native receiver and digital decoder as the scan reaches each channel type.
- Use a minimum dwell of 2.5 seconds per memory to give digital signals time to acquire.
- Hold on analog activity and integrated-decoder calls; show the analog signal level above nearby spectrum noise.

## Reduce false analog scan holds

- Ignore the RTL-SDR center/DC spur for voice modes and require activity across multiple channel bins before holding.
- Require stronger signal evidence at the fastest scan speed so isolated FFT spikes do not stop the scan as easily.
